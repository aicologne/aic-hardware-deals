Methodology & notes

    📖 Glossary (quick start)
    • RDIMM — registered server RAM (ECC); cheap, abundant used, fits X99/EPYC boards, not normal desktops.
    • ECC — error-correcting memory; server-grade reliability for long-running AI jobs.
    • VRAM — GPU memory; the real bottleneck for local AI (16 GB+ recommended).
    • X99 / LGA2011-3 — cheap used platform (Xeon E5-v4) with lots of RAM slots; the budget AI-workstation path.
    • Buy-low target — the price window a category is scanned for; 🔥 flags listings within 15 % of it.
    • €/GB — price ÷ capacity, the value metric for GPUs and RAM (see the tables).
